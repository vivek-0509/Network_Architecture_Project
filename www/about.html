bhttp/1

This page was served by bserve over bhttp/1, a binary framing of HTTP: an 8-byte fixed header, three frame types, and header names looked up in a ten-entry static table.

Every byte of the exchange that fetched /index.html is annotated in HEXDUMP.md; the wire format is in SPEC.md.

Other things to fetch: /docs/ (a directory, served as its index.html), /hello.txt, and /missing (a 404).
